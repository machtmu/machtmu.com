# Igniter Instrumentation Test - June 24th, 2022

This is the earliest completed test of the team's own hardware found in the surviving archive. A custom Arduino-based data-acquisition box recorded thermocouple and electrical measurements from an igniter burn.

## Result

The processed record reported a peak temperature of 1,368 °C and about 9.17 seconds above 1,000 °C. These are historical processed values, not a new analysis of the raw sensor signal.
