# Launch Canada LR-101 Firing Support - October 24th, 2021

Ryerson Propulsion Group, MACH's predecessor, sent members to help Launch Canada assemble and operate its LR-101 test system in Welland. The LR-101 engine and test campaign belonged to Launch Canada; this was not an RPG engine firing.

## Firing result

Launch Canada's test video records a 35-second firing and reports a peak thrust of 1,000 lbf, approximately 4.45 kN. The Drive archive files the campaign under October 23rd and 24th, 2021, and the firing photographs are dated October 24th. The video's title card says “Sunday October 21, 2021,” but October 21st was a Thursday.
