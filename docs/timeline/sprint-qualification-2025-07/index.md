# SPRINT Structural and Piston Qualification - July 2025

The 2025 Launch Canada report records completed load checks of the SPRINT support hardware and a separate pressure hold behind the tank piston. The report does not give the individual test dates. The team record places these tests after SPRINT began in July 2025.

## Structural tests

The tank tripod carried three times its expected load. A calibrated hydraulic ram applied 500 lbf at the test stand's engine flange, twice its nominal load. Its fasteners were then checked after ten cycles from 0 to 250 lbf; no M8 or M10 fasteners loosened.

## Piston pressure hold

The stacked tank's piston held 800 psig during its documented functional test. The same report lists 1,335 psig as the design proof target for the complete tank; it does not document that target as an achieved test pressure.
