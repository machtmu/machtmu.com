      <h2>Ethanol and nitrous oxide CDR</h2>
      <p>The proof-of-concept design targeted 1.56 kN thrust, a 500 psi chamber, a 10 s burn, 750 psi run tanks and 0.795 kg/s total propellant flow. It used an 80-channel impinging injector with regenerative and film cooling. The CDR placed component flow, cold-flow and hot-fire testing in future work.</p>
