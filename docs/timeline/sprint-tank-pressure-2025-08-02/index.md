# SPRINT Suspended-Hardware Pressure Test - August 2nd, 2025

The Structures channel scheduled a tank pressure test on July 28th and described suspending the tank from the tripod. A propulsion-channel result posted at 12:10 a.m. on August 2nd states “Successful pressure test” and shows the suspended SPRINT tank beside its ground-support controls.

The scheduling and photographs support a tank-related test, but the result does not identify which internal volume or pressure boundary was tested. It also does not state the applied pressure or hold duration. This event is separate from the earlier 800 psig piston verification documented in the Launch Canada report, and the report's 1,335 psig complete-tank proof target cannot be treated as the achieved pressure. An August 3rd task list still called for pressure-testing the engine, so this result was not an engine test.
