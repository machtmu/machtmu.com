# GAR-E V2 Cold Flow Test - July 14th, 2024

This test characterized the new GAR-E injector and thrust chamber, checked system compatibility and measured the cavitating venturi discharge coefficient.

The second test recorded an average fuel mass flow of 0.189 kg/s over 6.364 seconds. The measured cavitating venturi discharge coefficient was 0.856, compared with 0.860 from the previous characterization. The fuel injector pressure drop was approximately 108 psi with an injector discharge coefficient of 0.367.
