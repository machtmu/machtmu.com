# GAR-E Hot Fire Test - August 22nd, 2024

GAR-E completed a successful nitrous oxide and ethanol hot fire at Launch Canada 2024. The following year's PDR describes GAR-E as a 300 lbf liquid rocket engine and identifies this test as validation of the propellant, thrust-chamber and ignition approach.
