# Structures

## About the Structures Team

The Structures team shapes the backbone of the rocket by designing and fabricating frames, mounts, and airframes. The team contributes to building test stands, tripods, and composites while verifying strength, fit, and safety. This team specializes in hands-on design, manufacturing, materials engineering, and structural analysis to ensure the rocket's mechanical integrity.

## System Overview

The Structures subsystem encompasses all mechanical framework components that provide structural support and mounting interfaces for the SPRINT system. This includes the rocket airframe, engine mount structures, recovery bay assemblies, and ground support equipment frames.

## Design Philosophy

Our structural designs prioritize:
- **Safety**: All structures must withstand expected loads with appropriate safety factors
- **Manufacturability**: Designs optimized for available fabrication methods and tools
- **Integration**: Seamless interfaces with other subsystems
- **Testing**: Structures support comprehensive ground testing requirements
