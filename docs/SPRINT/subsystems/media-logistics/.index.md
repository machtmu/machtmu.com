# Media & Logistics

## About the Media & Logistics Team

The Media & Logistics team keeps the mission moving through outreach, safety, and operations. The team runs events, manages social media, fosters sponsor relations, and oversees inventory, transport, and paperwork. This team combines communication, organization, and problem-solving to support engineering success and promote the team's achievements.

## System Overview

The Media & Logistics subsystem encompasses all non-technical operations that enable successful rocket development and testing. This includes documentation, safety protocols, equipment management, and public outreach activities.

## Key Responsibilities

### Media & Communications
- **Documentation**: Technical writing, photography, and video production
- **Social Media**: Content creation and community engagement
- **Outreach**: Public presentations and educational activities
- **Sponsor Relations**: Partnership development and maintenance

### Operations & Safety
- **Safety Management**: Risk assessment and safety protocol implementation
- **Logistics Coordination**: Equipment transport, inventory tracking, and test site preparation
- **Event Management**: Test day coordination and public demonstrations
- **Administrative Support**: Paperwork, permits, and regulatory compliance
