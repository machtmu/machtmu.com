# SPRINT Hot Fire Test - December 16th and 17th, 2025

For this hotfire we testing in about -5 degrees C. The nitrous supply tank was too cold so we had to use our tank heater. We wrapped it in a fibre glass blanket. It took about an hour to raise the pressure from 400 to 800 psi. The actual rocket tank was not heated it ended up cooling the nitrous way too much to about 350psi. This resulted in way less thrust. Since the nitrous was cooled it flowed faster through the injector. The system ran ox rich and the fuel wasn't depleted. So when we went to do our nitrogen purge, the fuel was then pushed out of the system resulting in a minor fire.
