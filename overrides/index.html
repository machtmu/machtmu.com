About Mach

MACH is TMU's liquid rocketry team, pushing the boundaries of aerospace engineering through hands-on design and testing. We're developing cutting-edge liquid propulsion engines, advanced avionics systems, and conducting real-world test campaigns that prepare students for careers in the space industry.

Join our team of passionate engineers as we design PCB modules, test rocket engines, and compete in national rocketry competitions. Whether you're interested in mechanical design, electronics, software development, or project management, MACH offers opportunities to work on meaningful aerospace projects that make an impact.
