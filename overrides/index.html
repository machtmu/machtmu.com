About Mach

MACH is Toronto Metropolitan University's liquid rocketry team, also known as TMU Rocketry, pushing the boundaries of aerospace engineering through hands-on design and testing. We're developing cutting-edge liquid propulsion engines, advanced avionics systems, and conducting real-world test campaigns that prepare students for careers in the space industry.

Our team designs PCB modules, tests rocket engines, and competes in national rocketry competitions.
